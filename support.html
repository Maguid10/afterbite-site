Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Afterbite › Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new phone and Premium isn’t showing

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

The calories look wrong

Photo estimates depend on how much of the meal is visible. Tap the meal to change the portion or edit any value. For packaged food, scanning the barcode or the nutrition label gives exact numbers.

A scan failed

Check your internet connection and try again. If you scan many meals in a row, wait a minute: there’s a short limit to prevent abuse.

My workouts aren’t showing up from Apple Health

Make sure Profile › Apple Health is on, then open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › Afterbite, and allow Workouts. Afterbite imports workouts from the last 14 days each time you open it.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is stored on your iPhone. Tap Profile › Erase all data, or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
