Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

The short version

  • There’s no account. Your food log, photos, weight and profile are stored on your iPhone.
  • When you scan a meal, the photo or description is sent to our server to be analyzed and is not stored there.
  • Apple Health data stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps, no analytics, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains what data the Afterbite app (“Afterbite”, “we”, “us”) uses, why, and who it is shared with. If you have a question, write to maguidguebre10@gmail.com.

1. Data stored on your device

Afterbite doesn’t have accounts or a cloud database. The following is saved only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage:

  • Your profile and goals: name (optional), age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and the answers you give during setup.
  • Your log: meals and their photos, nutrition estimates, water, weight entries and workouts.
  • Your settings, such as reminders and units.

We can’t see this data. It’s removed when you tap Profile › Erase all data or delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

2. Meal analysis

When you scan a meal, read a nutrition label or describe what you ate, Afterbite sends the following to our server:

  • the photo and/or the text you typed;
  • your goal (for example “lose weight”), so the grade and suggestions fit it;
  • a random identifier created when you installed the app, used only to limit how many scans can be made per minute. It isn’t linked to your name, Apple ID or any other identifier.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver the request. The server passes the photo or text to Anthropic, whose AI model (Claude) produces the nutrition estimate, and sends the result back to your phone. We don’t store your photos, descriptions or results on the server. Anthropic processes the request as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use your data to train its models.

3. Food search and barcodes

When you search the food database or scan a barcode, the app sends your search words or the barcode number directly to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to look up nutrition facts. These services receive your IP address as part of the request. No other information is sent.

4. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Afterbite:

  • reads your workouts and the active energy they burned, to add them to your daily log;
  • writes the calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber and sugar of the meals you log, and your weight entries.

Health data is used only to provide these features. It stays on your iPhone: it is never sent to our server or to any third party, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app (your profile picture › Apps › Afterbite) and turn syncing off in Profile › Apple Health.

5. Camera and photos

Afterbite uses the camera only when you open the scanner, and reads only the photos you pick from your library. Photos are used to analyze your meal as described in section 2 and are saved in the app on your device.

6. Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for Afterbite and basic device information (such as device model, OS version, country and IP address).

7. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

8. What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t sell or rent your data.

9. Legal basis (EEA and UK)

We process the data in sections 2, 3 and 6 because it’s necessary to provide the features you ask for (performance of a contract), and the data in section 4 only with your explicit consent, which you give in iOS’s Health permission screen and can withdraw at any time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because your data is stored on your device, you can view, edit and delete it yourself in the app. For anything else, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Afterbite isn’t directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA) and we don’t knowingly process their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. If the change is significant, we’ll let you know in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: maguidguebre10@gmail.com
